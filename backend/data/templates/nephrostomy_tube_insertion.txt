Nephrostomy Tube Insertion Under Guide of Sonography:

After routine prep & drep, under guide of sonography, local anesthesia was done and nephrostomy tube (gaje VIII ) was inserted in Rt pelvicalyceal system and clear urine was aspirated and connected to bag.
No complications was seen during procedure.
