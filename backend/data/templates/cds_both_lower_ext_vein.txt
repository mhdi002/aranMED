Color Doppler sonography of both lower Extremities veins:
According to gray scale, color flow mapping and spectral analysis the study reveals:

Normal caliber of both side common femoral, superficial femoral and popliteal veins and also visualized parts of deep leg veins, with well compressibility and no definite clot within them.
Color flow mapping and spectral analysis shows normal flow pattern and direction, associated with well changing of waveform with respiration.
The calf muscle augmentation test is reactive. So there is no sign of D.V.T at the mentioned veins at present time.
No occlusion, also no sign of thrombosis or aneurysmal dilatation is seen.
Both side saphenofemoral venous junction show normal caliber flow direction with no flow reversal during valsalva maneuver, also no superficial varicose vein seen.
Conclusion: Normal color Doppler sonography of both lower extremities.
