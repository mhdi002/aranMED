Color doppler sonography Mesenteric vessels:
According to gray scale, color flow mapping and spectral analysis of main branches superior mesenteric artery and visualized part of celiac trunk and superior mesenteric vein, the study reveals:
Mentioned vessels has normal caliber with no definite plaque, thrombosis or occlusion. Color flow mapping shows normal flow encoding with no aliasing or flow reversal.
According to spectral analysis peak systolic velocity and end-diastolic velocities of SMA are within normal range and there is no significant rise of PSV of SMA.
Comparing to PSV of abdominal aorta (ratio < 3.5) so there is no sign of significant stenosis or occlusion at the mentioned arteries.
Visualized parts of superior mesenteric and splenic veins show normal caliber with normal hepatopetal flow, without any evidence of thrombosis.
